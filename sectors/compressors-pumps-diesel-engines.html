Sector Description

The Compressors, Pumps & Diesel Engines sector is a core capital goods sub-sector in India supplying critical industrial infrastructure: air compressors (ElGi, Ingersoll-Rand), fluid management systems (KSB, Kirloskar Brothers), diesel engines (Cummins, Kirloskar Oil, Swaraj), and specialised pump solutions (Shakti, Roto). The sector's fortunes are deeply tied to India's infrastructure capex cycle, manufacturing growth (Make in India), agricultural modernisation, power generation needs, and data centre expansion.

Industry Tailwinds (2025–2026 Update)

  • Diesel Engines: India's diesel engine market reached USD 9.4 billion in 2025, projected to grow at ~4.3% CAGR to USD 13.9 billion by 2034, driven by infrastructure construction, agricultural mechanisation, and backup power demand. CPCB IV+ emission norms are a structural tailwind for premium engine makers like Cummins and Kirloskar Oil.
  • Compressors: India's compressor market was at USD 1.87 billion in 2023 and projected at 6.6% CAGR through 2030, driven by automotive and manufacturing growth. ElGi and Ingersoll-Rand are key beneficiaries.
  • Data Centres: The data centre boom is a powerful new demand driver — Cummins India disclosed data centres contributed 25–30% of Power Generation (PG) segment revenue in H1 FY26, driven by hyperscaler buildouts.
  • Government Capex: PMKSY (irrigation), Jal Jeevan Mission (water supply), and national infrastructure programmes create durable order pipelines for pump companies (KSB, Kirloskar Brothers).
  • Export Recovery: Cummins India's exports grew 34% YoY in Q1 FY26; ElGi is expanding in USA, Australia, Middle East.

Industry Risks

  • CPCB IV+ compliance transition creates near-term sales lumpiness.
  • Chinese competition is emerging in compressors and gensets.
  • Hyperscaler data centre orders are inherently lumpy and may not repeat.
  • Kirloskar Oil, Greaves Cotton, and Shakti Pumps all show negative FCF over multiple periods — a systemic working capital concern in the mid-tier.
  • EV transition poses long-term threat to diesel engine demand (though 10+ year horizon).
