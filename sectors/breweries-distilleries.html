1. INDUSTRY OVERVIEW

Market Size & Growth

India's alcohol market is one of the largest and fastest-growing in the world. The market was valued at approximately USD 68–176 billion in 2025 (varying by scope of measurement — retail vs. wholesale vs. including country liquor). Industry sources converge on a CAGR of 5.75%–7.4% through 2034–35.

Total Beverage Alcohol Volume Growth (H1 2025) +7% YoY, crossing 440 mn 9-litre cases
India's rank in global alcohol market On track to become 5th largest by 2033

Key Growth Drivers (2025–2026)

  • Premiumization — The Prestige & Above (P&A) segment is outpacing mass-market growth. Radico Khaitan's P&A category grew 24% in Q2 FY26.
  • Urbanization — More than 40% of India's population is expected to be urban by 2030, driving social drinking culture.
  • Favorable Demographics — Legal drinking age population approaching 1 billion consumers.
  • 100% FDI under automatic route approved since February 2025 in brewing & distillation — attracting global players.
  • State Deregulation — Karnataka's AIB excise structure shift and UP Excise Policy 2026-27 (record ₹71,278 crore revenue target) signal progressive reform.
  • RTD & Craft Spirits — Fast-growing sub-segment; India's first crafted sparkling RTD cocktail ("Misfit" by Grover) launched Sept 2025. RTDs projected at CAGR +6% to 2034.
  • Indian Single Malts — Rampur, Paul John gaining global recognition; Pernod Ricard building Asia's largest malt distillery.

2. INDUSTRY RISKS & HEADWINDS

Risk Details
State Excise Volatility Maharashtra excise hike (June 2025) caused EBITDA drops up to 20%. Telangana supply disruption (Jan 2025).
Regulatory Fragmentation Each of India's 28 states has its own excise regime — pricing, licensing, quotas all vary.
Input Cost Inflation Glass bottles, PET packaging, ENA (Extra Neutral Alcohol) prices elevated due to geopolitical pressures.
High Valuations Several stocks trade at P/E 50–100x — vulnerable to earnings disappointments.
Competition from Global MNCs Diageo (via USL), Heineken (via UBL), Pernod Ricard, Carlsberg, AB-InBev all intensifying India focus.
Advertising Restrictions Surrogate advertising norms, potential stricter regulation.
Health Consciousness Growing segment of young consumers choosing low/no-alcohol alternatives.

3. ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK — STOCK SCREENING CRITERIA

The following multi-factor framework is applied to classify stocks:

Framework Pillars

  1. Profitability Quality — ROCE > 15% (indicates capital efficiency), ROE > 15% sustained.
  2. Free Cash Flow (FCF) Health — Positive FCF across 5-year, 3-year, and trailing annual; or improving trend.
  3. Valuation Comfort — P/E benchmarked against sector median (~40–50x).
  4. Scale & Moat — Market Cap as a proxy for pricing power, distribution reach, brand strength.
  5. Trend Momentum — Improving metrics (e.g. ROE improvement, FCF turning positive).

Classification Labels

  • 🟢 BUY / Watchlist (Strong) — Solid fundamentals + reasonable valuation + positive FCF.
  • 🟡 WATCHLIST (Monitor) — Good fundamentals but expensive valuation or mixed FCF.
  • 🔴 AVOID — Weak fundamentals, negative FCF, or distressed financials.
  • ⚪ NEUTRAL / Small Cap — Too small or data insufficient for reliable analysis.

Market Cap: ₹96,061 Cr | P/E: 52.6x | ROCE: 27.49% | ROE: 21.41%

Positives: - India's largest spirits company; subsidiary of Diageo PLC (global spirits giant). - Flagship brands: McDowell's No.1, Royal Challenge, Johnnie Walker, Black Dog, Don Julio (Tequila). - Stellar capital efficiency — ROCE of 27.49% and consistent ROE ~20%+ over 5 years. - Excellent FCF generation: ₹5,742 Cr over 5 years and ₹1,786 Cr in the previous year — best in class. - Ongoing premiumization: Launched Don Julio (tequila) nationally, X-Series (McDowell's), investing in Pistola (premium agave brand). - Strong operating leverage as premium mix improves.

Concerns: - P/E of 52.6x — elevated; growth expectations are priced in. - Maharashtra excise hike could affect near-term margins.

Verdict: 🟢 WATCHLIST — Strong Core Holding

Best-in-class FCF, ROCE, and brand moat. Expensive but justified for long-term investors. Add on dips below ₹1,200.

8. KEY THEMES TO TRACK IN FY27

  1. Karnataka Excise Reform Execution — Full implementation of AIB structure; watch for volume data from South India companies (Tilaknagar, UBL, United Spirits).

  6. RTD and New Category Launches — Track innovation pipeline across players.
  7. Pernod Ricard India Performance — Their India organic growth (11% YoY) is a positive leading indicator for the sector.
