INDUSTRY CONTEXT: SPECIALTY CARBON CHEMICALS

Sector definition: Both companies are carbon black / speciality chemical manufacturers deriving feedstock from coal tar distillates and petroleum feedstock. Their products are essential intermediates across tyres, batteries, paints, graphite electrodes, aluminium smelting, and construction.

Industry economics (outside view): - Carbon black is a semi-commodity with periodic margin compression when crude derivatives are expensive and demand is soft - The industry typically earns 12–20% ROCE for volume players; specialty and innovation-led companies can sustainably earn 20–35% - China is the world's largest carbon black producer, but quality/regulatory standards give Indian specialty players a structural moat in serving European and domestic tire OEM markets - Battery materials is the high-growth adjacency being chased by both companies — LFP cathode material, anode materials, and specialty carbon black for EV battery applications - India's EV battery market is expected to reach ~$19.5 billion by 2030, supporting a multi-year tailwind for battery-grade specialty carbons and cathode/anode materials

Structural risk factor (industry level): Carbon black demand from traditional tyres is a 15–20 year secular theme. Battery materials is the high-growth overlay, but also brings technology risk, China competition, and raw material complexity. The "outside view" industry ROCE is approximately 12–18% for commodity carbon black, rising to 20–30%+ for specialty and battery-grade products.

Verdict: ✅ PASS — Promoter integrity is strong. Zero pledge, rising promoter holding, and consistent alignment with shareholder interests.

Filter 1.2 — Turnaround Check

Is Himadri a turnaround story? On surface, the FY22 data looks alarming (ROCE 4.6%, PAT ₹39 Cr). However, this requires careful reading:

  • FY22 was a cyclical trough — coal tar prices and energy costs spiked globally during the Russia-Ukraine supply disruption, compressing margins for all carbon chemistry companies
  • The business model itself (backward integration, proprietary coal tar processing, R&D-led product mix) did not change between FY21 and FY22
  • There was no management change, no restructuring, and no regulatory windfall
  • FY23+ recovery was driven by restored spreads + volume expansion + product mix upgrade — i.e., the same business doing the same thing with normalised input costs
  • The LFP cathode/battery materials push is an organic forward integration of existing carbon chemistry expertise — not a pivot by a distressed management

This is not a turnaround. It is a cyclically-impacted specialty chemical company whose underlying economics were intact and have since re-established themselves with a strengthened balance sheet.

Verdict: ✅ PASS — Not a turnaround. Temporary margin compression due to external commodity shock; underlying quality was never impaired.

Filter 1.5 — Industry Pace of Change

Two-layer analysis:

Layer A — Core business (coal tar pitch, carbon black, naphthalene): - This chemistry has been commercially stable for 50+ years - Products serve structural industries: aluminium smelters, tyre manufacturers, graphite electrode producers, construction chemicals - No technology disruption threat in the 10-year horizon for coal tar pitch - SLOW-CHANGING core ✅

Layer B — Battery materials (anode, LFP cathode): - This IS a fast-evolving technology landscape: LFP vs NMC vs sodium-ion debates, Chinese players 2+ generations ahead, large capital requirements - LFP is the dominant chemistry in 2+ wheelers and grid storage — but the technology could be disrupted by sodium-ion or solid-state batteries within 10–15 years - Himadri's first commercial LFP cathode production is only targeted at 2,000 MTPA in Q3 FY27 — the business is pre-revenue in this segment - HIGH pace of change in the battery materials layer ⚠️

Mitigation: The core coal tar / carbon black business constitutes ~93–95% of current earnings. Battery materials are effectively a funded call option. The company is NOT primarily a battery materials company yet. The Darwinian risk would increase if battery materials became >30% of capital employed before proving stable returns.

Verdict: ✅ PASS (with monitoring flag) — Core business is slow-changing. Battery materials overlay brings future disruption risk but is currently a minor capital deployment with management explicitly stating ROCE discipline governs the rollout.
