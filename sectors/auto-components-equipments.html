A2. Structural Industry Assessment

What this industry sells:
Auto components covers a very wide spectrum — from ICE-only parts (pistons, fuel injectors, exhaust systems) to powertrain-agnostic parts (bearings, wiring harnesses, wheels, glass, fasteners, forgings) to EV-enhancing parts (power electronics, BMS adjacent, thermal management).

This distinction is critical for Gate 1.5 (Industry Pace of Change).

A3. Key Industry Risks (Darwin Gate 1.5 Lens)

Bullish structural factors (Darwin-safe): - India remains a predominantly ICE market for the foreseeable decade; EV penetration at 8.4% with ICE as "mainstay" (ACMA DG, Feb 2026) - Aftermarket demand is powertrain-agnostic and growing - India–US and India–EU trade engagement boosting export opportunity - PLI scheme doubled to ₹5,940 crore in Union Budget FY27

Risk factors (monitored, not disqualifying at industry level): - EV transition will commoditise ICE-specific powertrain parts over a 10–15 year horizon - China's auto component exports remain ~30% cheaper in segments like tyres and glass - Ocean freight volatility (Red Sea route) adds 2–3x cost pressure on exporters/importers - Only 30–40% of EV supply chain is localised — creating new China import dependency risk

Darwin Conclusion on Industry Pace of Change:
The industry is NOT fast-changing in the social-media/crypto sense. ICE dominance in India persists. However, companies whose entire revenue base is ICE powertrain-specific face a structural 10-year headwind. The Darwin framework demands we avoid those. Companies with powertrain-agnostic products are structurally safe.
