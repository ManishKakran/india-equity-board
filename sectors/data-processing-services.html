🏭 INDUSTRY OVERVIEW — DATA PROCESSING SERVICES (INDIA)

What Is This Sub-Sector?

The "Data Processing Services" classification on Indian exchanges covers a heterogeneous group of companies engaged in software-enabled information processing — ranging from B2B market intelligence platforms (like Tracxn) to small-cap web services/digital marketing firms (like Megri Soft). These are NOT large-scale IT services companies; they occupy a niche segment of the broader ₹2.76 trillion (~$33B) India Software market.

Industry Verdict for Darwin Framework

  • Market Intelligence SaaS (Tracxn's segment): Structurally attractive but faces intense competition from well-capitalised global peers (Crunchbase, CB Insights, PitchBook). Network effects matter. AI disruption is a real risk.
  • Web Services/Digital Agencies (Megri's segment): Commoditised. Low barriers to entry. Structural pricing pressure. Industry economics do not support sustained high ROCE.

🔬 COMPANY 1: TRACXN TECHNOLOGIES (NSE: TRACXN)

BACKGROUND

Founded in 2012 by Neha Singh (CEO, ex-Sequoia Capital) and Abhishek Goyal (ex-Accel Partners), both IIT alumni. Tracxn is a B2B SaaS platform providing private company market intelligence — covering 5M+ companies, 60M+ legal entities across 50+ countries. Clients include VC/PE funds, investment banks, corporate M&A teams, and Fortune 500 companies. Listed on BSE/NSE in October 2022 (IPO was entirely an OFS at ₹80/share).

Key recent development: In October 2025, Tracxn signed a strategic agreement with TMX Datalinx (information arm of TMX Group) to become the exclusive Canadian provider of private market intelligence to financial institutions — a meaningful enterprise expansion signal.

⛩️ GATE 1 — AVOID BIG RISKS

Filter 1.1 — Promoter / Management Integrity

  • Promoters: Neha Singh (MD) and Abhishek Goyal (Director) — both ex-top-tier VC professionals with clean public track records.
  • Promoter holding: 34.87% as of 2024. This is below the preferred >40–50% but above the minimum 25% threshold.
  • Pledging: No evidence of promoter share pledging. HDFC Bank charge of ₹1.55 Cr (registered Oct 2022) was fully satisfied in October 2024 — positive signal.
  • Regulatory actions: No SEBI/NCLT/court actions found. Company is compliant per MCA records.
  • IPO Structure: The IPO was 100% OFS by promoters and early investors — promoters sold down at ₹80 (stock is now ₹33). While this is not fraud, it raises the question of founder confidence at listing price.
  • Board refresh concern: As per Simply Wall St, no new directors have joined the board in 3 years — flagged as insufficient board refreshment.
  • Independent Director resignation: Nishant Verman resigned effective March 31, 2026 — a minor governance concern.

Verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS — No fraud or integrity breach found. However, promoters selling heavily at IPO and the stock trading at a 59% discount to IPO price warrants caution. Director resignation is a minor yellow flag.

Filter 1.2 — Turnaround Check

  • FY2025 performance: Revenue grew 9.2% to ₹90.4 Cr, but the company posted a net loss of ₹9.54 Cr — a swing from profit in FY2024. EPS turned to -₹0.89 from +₹0.61.
  • The loss was driven by higher expenses — suggesting the company is spending (on sales, headcount, or product) ahead of revenue growth.
  • Historical trajectory: Tracxn went public claiming profitability, but losses have re-emerged. ROCE of 7.9% is well below the cost of capital.
  • Is this a turnaround thesis? Partially. The company is not in persistent multi-year loss, but the FY2025 regression to losses after a brief profitable FY2024 window raises the turnaround flag.
  • Thesis dependency: Bulls would argue the TMX deal and global expansion create a path to profitability. This is a future event thesis — exactly what Nalanda rejects.

Verdict: ⚠️ BORDERLINE — LEAN DISQUALIFY The FY2025 swing to losses after one profitable year, combined with sub-cost-of-capital ROCE, means the investment thesis requires assuming a future financial turnaround. Nalanda's framework explicitly rejects such bets.

Under Darwin rules, a company failing to demonstrate sustained historical excellence must be disqualified. We will flag and continue for completeness, but this is a serious concern.

Filter 1.3 — Debt / Leverage Test

  • Debt-to-Equity: 0.00 across all available years (FY2022–FY2025) — zero financial debt.
  • Current Ratio: 2.43 (TTM), 2.48 (FY2025) — healthy liquidity.
  • Cash position: Net Current Asset Value ₹53.7 Cr (FY2025). Cash ratio 0.09 — modest cash buffer.
  • HDFC Bank charge of ₹1.55 Cr was operating in nature and fully settled.

Verdict: ✅ PASS — Debt-free balance sheet. This is a genuine strength.

Filter 1.4 — Serial Acquirer Check

  • No evidence of acquisitions in Tracxn's operating history. The company is organically built.
  • Growth has come from expanding subscription base and geographic reach — organic.
  • No goodwill on balance sheet from M&A.

Verdict: ✅ PASS

Filter 1.5 — Industry Pace of Change

  • HIGH DISRUPTION RISK. This is the most significant structural concern for Tracxn.
  • The private market intelligence industry is undergoing rapid transformation with generative AI — OpenAI, Perplexity, and others can synthesise startup data on demand.
  • Global competitors (Crunchbase, CB Insights, PitchBook, Dealroom) are well-capitalised and integrating AI aggressively. Dealroom secured new funding as recently as January 2026.
  • The "human analyst + technology" model that Tracxn employs may face commoditisation as LLMs get better at data aggregation.
  • Industry rulebook is rewriting itself every 2–3 years due to AI — a clear Nalanda disqualifier.
  • On the other hand, proprietary structured databases (60M+ legal entities, financials of private companies) with human curation could be a defensible moat if AI enhances rather than replaces the data layer.
  • Morningstar rates Tracxn as trading at a 898% premium to estimated fair value — suggesting the market still prices in some moat, though its durability is questionable.

Verdict: ⚠️ BORDERLINE FAIL — The data intelligence industry faces technological disruption from AI within the 5–10 year horizon. This is not a "slow-changing industry" by Nalanda's definition.

Filter 1.6 — Ownership Alignment

  • Promoter holding: 34.87% (FY2024) — above the 25% minimum but below ideal 40–50%.
  • Founders are the largest shareholders and active in management (Neha Singh as MD).
  • Investor overhang risk: Elevation Capital has been actively selling (sold 15+ lakh shares for ₹15 Cr). Accel and Sequoia also partial sellers at IPO. Continued institutional selling is a price risk.
  • Insider buying: No meaningful insider buying post-IPO found.

Verdict: ✅ PASS (borderline) — Founders retain control and are active in management. No principal-agent problem.

🔬 COMPANY 2: MEGRI SOFT (BSE: 539012)

BACKGROUND

Incorporated on February 5, 1992 and registered with STPI as a 100% Software Export Oriented Unit. Chandigarh-based. Provides web design, mobile app development, SEO, digital marketing, e-commerce solutions, and CMS development. Has a 100% subsidiary — Megrisoft Limited — registered in England and Wales. Revenue is approximately ₹2.7–3.4 Cr per year. Total employees: ~23 (per PitchBook, 34 per earlier data). Essentially a micro-cap web agency.

⛩️ GATE 1 — AVOID BIG RISKS

Filter 1.1 — Promoter / Management Integrity

  • Company is registered as STPI unit — implies some level of regulatory oversight and compliance
  • Size-related risk: At ₹26 Cr market cap, this is a micro-cap with minimal institutional coverage — hard to assess integrity comprehensively

Verdict: ✅ CONDITIONAL PASS — No active integrity concerns found; promoter stake is high and stable.

Filter 1.2 — Turnaround Check

  • Is this a turnaround? Not quite — the company has been marginally profitable for years but has never demonstrated the scale or quality to qualify as a "high-quality business."
  • FY2025 performance: Revenue declining quarter-over-quarter. Q1 FY2026 (Apr–Jun 2025) revenue ₹0.72 Cr vs. ₹1.19 Cr in Q1 FY2025 — a 39.5% revenue decline YoY. This is alarming.
  • Q3 FY2026 (Oct–Dec 2025): Revenue ₹0.62 Cr vs. ₹0.68 Cr — continuing decline
  • Net profit is tiny and shrinking: ₹0.09–0.15 Cr per quarter — sub-₹1 Cr annually
  • ROCE of 3.69% is deeply sub-standard and declining

Verdict: ⚠️ BORDERLINE FAIL — Not a classic turnaround story, but a structurally declining micro-business with deteriorating revenues and no visible growth catalyst.

Filter 1.3 — Debt / Leverage Test

Verdict: ✅ PASS — Zero debt. Clean balance sheet.

Filter 1.4 — Serial Acquirer Check

  • No goodwill entries of concern.

Verdict: ✅ PASS

Filter 1.5 — Industry Pace of Change

  • CRITICAL FAIL. Web design, SEO, and digital marketing are perhaps the most commoditised and fast-changing service categories in technology.

Verdict: ❌ HARD FAIL — The industry has already begun obsoleting this business model. This is not a risk; it is happening in real-time as evidenced by the revenue collapse.
