Key Structural Tailwinds

  • India has the world's largest working-age population driving savings and financialisation
  • UPI/digital onboarding has compressed account-opening friction dramatically
  • SIP culture is deeply embedded — monthly SIP inflows at ₹25,000+ crore run-rate
  • Shift from physical assets (gold, real estate) to financial assets structurally underway
  • B30 cities growing faster than T30, widening the addressable market

Key Regulatory Development (April 1, 2026)

SEBI introduced the Base Expense Ratio (BER) framework under SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026: - TER unbundled into: BER (AMC management fee) + brokerage + statutory levies (GST, STT, stamp duty shown separately) - Equity fund expense caps lowered ~10bps (less than the feared 15bps) - For large AMCs: broadly earnings-neutral — GST exclusion offsets lower headline caps - Brokerage caps halved: 6bps (cash) and 2bps (derivatives) - Exit load 5bps extra allowance removed - Net impact: relief rally in AMC stocks; no material earnings change expected for top-4 AMCs

Darwin Checklist — Buy Signals to Watch For

The top-4 AMCs are all exceptional businesses. The only barrier is price. Monitor for:

  • Broad market correction of 25–35% — would bring ICICI/HDFC/Nippon into watchable PE range
  • Aditya Birla at ₹700–750 — Darwin buy zone (16–17x TTM PE); first in line
  • Canara Robeco below ₹190–200 — 18–20x TTM PE; niche opportunity for smaller allocation
  • UTI AMC ROCE improvement above 20% — watch 2 annual report cycles; if ROCE crosses threshold, reassess

When to avoid selling (if already invested in ICICI/HDFC/Nippon):

  • ✗ Stock has risen further — not a sell signal
  • ✗ Market correction — not a sell signal
  • ✗ Quarterly earnings miss — not a sell signal
  • ✗ A "better opportunity" appears — not a sell signal
  • ✓ SELL ONLY IF: proven promoter fraud / catastrophic acquisition / structural industry obsolescence / permanent competitive impairment

Key Risks to Monitor — Sector-Wide

  1. TER compression (ongoing): SEBI may revisit BER caps further. Large AMCs most resilient; smaller players squeaky.
  2. Passive migration: ETF + index funds at ₹12.4 lakh crore — rising. Compresses yield per AUM over time for active managers. Top AMCs diversifying into passives as hedge.
  3. Fintech distribution disruption: Platforms like Zerodha, Groww gaining MFD market share. Reduces AMC pricing power on distribution costs.
  4. Market correction risk: 60%+ of equity AUM is mark-to-market. A 30% Sensex fall = ~18–20% AUM fall (with inflow offset) = direct revenue compression.
  5. Key man risk: Fund manager exits can trigger AUM outflows. ICICI Prudential and HDFC have deeper benches — lower risk.
