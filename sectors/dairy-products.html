🔑 Key Industry Trends (2025–2026)

  1. Value-Added Products (VAP) Surge — Curd, paneer, ghee, ice cream, cheese, probiotic yogurt gaining rapid share. Ice cream demand now spreads year-round rather than summer peaks.
  2. Tight Supply / Margin Squeeze — Post the Oct 2024–Mar 2025 flush season (milk output +25%), supply has tightened again in late 2025 due to unseasonal rains, India-Pakistan geopolitical tensions affecting Punjab/Haryana/J&K milk belts, and strong festive demand depleting inventories.
  3. GST Cut Tailwind — Recent GST reduction has boosted volumes (especially small SKUs) but compressed margins as consumer prices fell while procurement costs rose.
  4. Procurement Cost Pressure — Regional price hikes of ₹1–1.5/litre in Bihar and Andhra Pradesh; procurement cost corrections expected around April 2026.
  5. Premiumization & D2C — Subscription-based direct-to-consumer startups bypassing traditional distributors. A2 milk is the fastest-growing segment.
  6. FSSAI 2025 Certification — New quality standards favour organized, accredited processors over unorganized players — a structural advantage for listed companies.
  7. Rural Volume Growth — Rural demand outpacing urban for 3+ consecutive quarters.
  8. Africa Expansion — Dodla Dairy scaling Uganda operations aggressively (+48% Africa revenue in FY26).

⚠️ Key Risks (Current)

  • Raw milk procurement cost volatility (primary risk for all players)
  • Climate/weather disruptions to milk supply belts
  • Geopolitical events affecting northern dairy states
  • Limited pricing power vs cooperatives (Amul sets implicit price ceiling)
  • Capital-intensive cold chain infrastructure requirements
